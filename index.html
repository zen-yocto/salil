Chapter in Springer Proceedings

“Observation and Control of Smart Grid Using IoT and Cloud Technology”

Abstract

Traditional electrical grids have one-way communication and are centered around the distributor. They also lack routing and interconnection of different types of generation, which reduces the reliability of supply. To overcome this, we propose a comprehensive Smart Grid architecture with observation and control using the Internet of Things (IoT) and Cloud Technology. We integrated different energy sources (thermal and renewable) into one system and introduced automatic routing of electric power according to peak and low demand. It also includes smart metering using IoT and cloud technology, which gathers, sends and receives real-time data and provides two-way communication of power consumption between customer and producer. All data can be stored, accessed and analyzed on a secured, customized API platform. We built and thoroughly tested a demonstration model with a Wi-Fi-based smart meter, which helped us monitor and control electric power and improve the reliability of the system.
